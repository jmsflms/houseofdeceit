Live the game

Think you could survive a weekend of deceit?

House of Deceit is a real life Traitors-inspired experience in Whitstable, Kent, around one hour from London. Your group takes over the house for two nights while secret roles, challenges, murders and trials turn the whole weekend into one continuous game.

Unlike a short theatre or escape-room session, you play only with your own friends and stay inside the story from arrival until the final reckoning.

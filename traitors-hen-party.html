Why it works for a hen weekend

The game gives the weekend a shared story without forcing everyone through a timetable of unrelated activities. It creates natural conversation between friends who may not know one another well, while competitive players have plenty to fight over.

The experience is fully hosted, so the organiser does not have to write rules, assign roles or persuade the group to keep the game moving.

What should the organiser arrange?

Choose the date, confirm a group of at least 10 and secure the stay with one place. The house includes a large self-catering kitchen, and welcome pizzas are provided on the first evening. Drinks, later meals and any time in Whitstable can be organised around the game.

Does everybody have to perform?

No. Some guests will throw themselves into speeches, accusations and secret deals; quieter guests can observe, investigate and vote. The structure gives everyone a reason to participate without requiring costumes or prepared characters.

What happens if somebody is murdered?

They remain part of the weekend. House of Deceit is designed so that the game can change without removing a guest from the experience.

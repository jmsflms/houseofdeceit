Netflix etc. can be used with your own account. Please leave the remotes in the room. The large sofa can also be used as a spare bed.

Dining & garden

The dining room seats 15 and has a Smart TV. Patio doors lead to the garden.

Leave the patio-door key in the lock. To lock it, lift the handle while turning the key. There is one step to the patio and four steps down to the garden. Garden lights normally come on around sunset and switch off at 10pm.

Noise and curfews

Please don’t be noisy in the garden or hot tub after midnight Friday/Saturday or 10:30pm on weekdays. The cinema, dining and games rooms are better for late-night chats.

Kitchen & food

Cooking: 1 electric oven, 3 gas ovens, microwave, large air fryer/pressure cooker and gas hob. If the range hasn’t been used recently, gas can take a while to reach it. The electric oven tends to run cool, so check the thermometer inside.

Cleaning: two dishwashers. Cold storage: American fridge-freezer in the kitchen plus an overspill fridge. Cutlery is under the electric oven/microwave; dinnerware and glassware are in the glass cabinets. Please use plastic glasses outside and around the hot tub.

Milk: we don’t provide milk because the house is sometimes empty for several days.

Coffee machine: mainly intended for large pots of filter coffee. The reusable filter needs no paper. Bring your preferred coffee for the espresso function.

Hot tub

The hot tub is cleaned before and after every visit and treated with swimming-pool chemicals.

Curfew: midnight Friday/Saturday; 10:30pm weekdays.

The water is left cooler between visits. Use the arrows beside the display to set the temperature and allow an hour or two to warm up. The other buttons control the jets and lights.

Please shower off lotions first and never take glass into the hot tub. If the water falls below the jets, they may shoot water out. Use the hose to top it up.

Other house details

Gas central heating is controlled by the Nest thermostat in the ground-floor hallway. Electric heaters, extra blankets and cushions can also be found around the house.

Bed linen, indoor/outdoor towels and hair dryers are provided. The iron and ironing board are in the upstairs laundry. The washing machine adds detergent and conditioner automatically; TurboWash with 1400 spin is recommended.

Outside bins are at the side/front of the house. Red: paper. Blue: plastic, glass and metal. Black: general waste. Please tell us about breakages or stains so we can fix them. No smoking.

Before you leave

  • Strip beds you have used and leave the linen on each bed.
  • Place used towels in a bath.
  • Load and run the dishwasher.
  • Take rubbish to the bins at the side of the house, to the left as you leave the front door.
  • Red bin: paper. Blue bin: plastic, glass and metal. Black bin: general waste.
  • Check the back door is locked and all lights are off.
  • Turn down the hot-tub temperature.
  • Return the front-door key to the lockbox.

Sunday night / Monday morning: it is very helpful if you can put the bins out on the street.

Cinema room

Screen: up/down box on wall.
White remote: projector.
Black remote: Amazon Firestick.

Netflix etc. can be used with your own account. Please leave the remotes in the room. The large sofa can also be used as a spare bed.

Dining & garden

The dining room seats 15 and has a Smart TV. Patio doors lead to the garden.

Leave the patio-door key in the lock. To lock it, lift the handle while turning the key. There is one step to the patio and four steps down to the garden.

Kitchen

Cooking: 1 electric oven, 3 gas ovens, microwave, air fryer/pressure cooker and gas hob. The gas hobs may take time to light.

Cleaning: two dishwashers. Cold storage: American fridge-freezer in the kitchen plus an overspill fridge in the pantry. Cutlery is under the electric oven/microwave; dinnerware and glassware are in the glass cabinets. Please use plastic glasses outside and around the hot tub.

Hot tub

The hot tub is cleaned before and after every visit and treated with swimming-pool chemicals.

Curfew: midnight Friday/Saturday; 10:30pm weekdays.

The water is left cooler between visits. Use the arrows beside the display to set the temperature and allow an hour or two to warm up. The other buttons control the jets and lights.

If the water falls below the jets, they may shoot water out. Use the hose to top it up.

Other house details

Gas central heating is controlled by the Nest thermostat in the ground-floor hallway. Electric heaters, extra blankets and cushions can also be found around the house.

Bed linen is provided. Towels and hair dryers are in the closet. The iron and ironing board are in the upstairs laundry. The house has Trooli superfast fibre Wi-Fi. No smoking.

Before you leave

  • Strip beds you have used.
  • Place used towels in the bath.
  • Load and run the dishwasher.
  • Take rubbish to the bins at the side of the house, to the left as you leave the front door.
  • Red bin: paper. Blue bin: plastic, glass and metal. Black bin: general waste.
  • Check the back door is locked, all lights are off and the hot-tub temperature is turned down.

Sunday night / Monday morning: it is very helpful if you can put the bins out on the street.
